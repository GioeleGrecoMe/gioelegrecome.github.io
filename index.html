What I do

Spatial & immersive audio

Sound-field reconstruction, Ambisonics and 6DoF rendering from a few microphones.

Acoustic measurements & datasets

Campaigns in theatres and concert halls, spherical arrays up to 64 channels, open RIR datasets.

Localization & array processing

Direction-of-arrival estimation and distributed localization with networks of microphone arrays.

Physical & data-driven models

FEM and transfer-matrix modeling of instruments, instrument directivity, neural networks and NMF for acoustic and biomedical signals.
