About

I study how sound lives in space

I am a researcher in acoustic signal processing at the Image and Sound Processing Lab of Politecnico di Milano, where I completed my Ph.D. in 2026 and now continue as a Research Fellow.

My work sits where physics and data meet: rebuilding a sound field from a few microphones, rendering spatial audio for a moving listener, localizing sources with networks of sensors and understanding how musical instruments radiate sound. I measure real rooms, from theatres to concert halls, and share what I build as papers, datasets, code and demos.

I am also a classical saxophonist, so musical acoustics is where my two backgrounds meet. I teach acoustics and enjoy helping students turn a curiosity into a project.

Core Expertise

Spatial & Immersive Audio

  • Ambisonics and spherical harmonics
  • Wide-area 6DoF rendering with DirAC
  • Parametric virtual microphones: direct sound, early reflections, reverberation

Acoustic Measurements

  • Measurement campaigns in theatres and concert halls
  • Log-sweep RIR extraction and multichannel Dante acquisition
  • Spherical, linear and planar arrays, up to 64 channels
  • Room acoustic metrics: T20/T30, EDT, C50/C80, DRR

Array Processing & Localization

  • Direction-of-arrival estimation in reverberant rooms
  • Distributed estimation in acoustic sensor networks
  • Sound field reconstruction from sparse measurements

Musical & Physical Acoustics

  • Musical-instrument directivity

  • Graph neural networks and mixture-of-experts
