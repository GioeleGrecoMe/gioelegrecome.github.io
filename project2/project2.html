ParaDER: Sound Field Reconstruction with Early Reflection Modeling

From early reverberation estimation for RIR reconstruction (IWSSPA 2024) to parametric virtual microphones with early reflection modeling (EURASIP JASMP 2026).

Abstract

Six-degrees-of-freedom audio in virtual reality, augmented reality and teleconferencing needs accurate reconstruction of the direct sound, the early reflections and the diffuse field, which is hard with few measurements. ParaDER (Parametric modeling of Direct sound, Early reflections and Reverberation) is a unified parametric framework that separates and reconstructs these three components from a minimal set of microphones. Because the pipeline uses only a few source and image-source parameters, it preserves the timing and direction of early reflections, key cues for depth and localization.

Method

  • 1 · Direct sound

    Sources are localized by a sparse, regularized optimization that yields low-order spherical-harmonic coefficients.

  • 2 · Early reflections

    The estimated RIR is segmented into a few early reflections, each modeled as an image source whose position and amplitude are fitted to the data.

  • 3 · Reverberation

    Direct and early parts are propagated analytically to virtual microphones; the remaining energy is synthesized as isotropic diffuse reverberation.

  • IWSSPA 2024 model

    Early reflections modeled as a sum of Gaussian pulses g(t) = a·exp(−(t−τ)²/2σ²), localized as virtual sources by combinatorial optimization.
